# Full Teaching Optimization

Load for Create, Redesign, and full Optimize, including requests to optimize, iterate, or
fix all to completion. Narrow deterministic repairs and explicitly presentation-only
migrations can finish as `fixed/verified` without this challenge.

Before choosing a Create design or certifying Optimize, maintain a novelty
ledger and run two independent challenges: pedagogy/mechanism, and
deletion-first/accessibility. These are distinct reviews of the design, not a
requirement to spawn subagents.

A candidate must materially change a causal interaction/representation,
scaffold sequence, feedback/mastery probe, or deletion-first simplification.
Cosmetic variants do not count. Compare viable candidates on correctness,
accessibility, mastery proxies, prerequisite burden, misconception recovery,
actions/controls, and time to insight. Record why an axis has no viable
alternative. Accept extra friction only for a material mastery/recovery gain
and record that tradeoff.

A viable new family or adopted candidate resets challenge saturation and
clean-pass counting. If a practical exploration cap is reached, report
`incomplete saturation`; do not certify optimization. Challenge the stable
teaching model before applying an optimization confidence label.

Use the [clean-pass and confidence rules](verification.md) before certification.
Preserve uncertainty and incomplete verification in the report.
