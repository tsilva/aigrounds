## PROJECT PURPOSE

AI Grounds helps people learn artificial intelligence concepts through interactive browser playgrounds where manipulating an idea makes its behavior and tradeoffs visible.

## PROJECT REQUIREMENTS

- Published playgrounds must let learners manipulate an AI concept and immediately observe how the relevant behavior or tradeoff changes.
- At viewport widths below 768 pixels, the product must show only a notice asking the learner to open AI Grounds on a desktop or laptop.
- The home experience must present published and planned lessons in the intended learning sequence.
- The home experience must clearly distinguish published lessons from planned lessons and let learners open every published lesson.
- Secrets used by server-backed learning assistance must never be exposed to browser clients.
- New playgrounds and material playground redesigns must follow the approved shared learning-page design system, with AI Guide access in the experiment rail rather than the top navigation.
- Each playground offers a test using reusable question widgets.
- A playground is marked passed only after a test attempt achieves 100% accuracy.
- Every test opening starts fresh; closing or reloading must discard the current attempt rather than resume it.
- Each playground maintains a varied question reservoir covering its learning objectives and avoids frequent repetition for each learner.
